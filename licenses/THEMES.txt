Colour themes
-------------

The editor's colour themes, other than Grizzly Dark, Grizzly Graphite and
Grizzly Light, take their colours from these published palettes
(crates/gz-ui/src/theme/themes.rs maps each onto the editor's colour roles).
Each is listed with its authors, its licence and where it is published.

The editor takes only their colours; no file of these themes is part of it. Their
names belong to their owners and are used only to say whose colours a theme
shows: Dracula is a registered trademark of Zeno Rocha, GitHub a trademark of
GitHub, Inc., and every other name its owner's. Grizzly Editor and its authors
are not affiliated with any of them, and none of them has endorsed it.
