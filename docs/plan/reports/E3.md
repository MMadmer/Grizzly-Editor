# E3 - one widget per job: the last widgets two surfaces drew each for themselves

## What moved into the kit (`gz-ui`; for `docs/UI_DESIGN.md` section 4)

- **`tabs.rs`: `PanelTab`, `TabStyle`, `TabResponse`, `tab_width`, `flat_widths`, `tab_strip`.** The one panel tab (26 pt
  strip, accent overline on the tab in front, hover wash, label cut with an ellipsis and reported to the audit, a cross
  where a tab can close). `TabStyle::Docked` is the Level dock's tab (rounded top corners, label from the left, cross on
  hover and on the front tab; the dock lays the tabs out and owns the menu and the drag-out); `TabStyle::Flat` is a
  panel's own strip (square, label centred), and `tab_strip(ui, salt, labels, &mut current)` lays flat tabs across
  the width, sharing it when the labels do not fit. Replaces `gz-ui-level`'s `local_tab` / `TabResponse` /
  `local_tab_width` and `gz-ui-pda`'s `local_tab_strip`.
- **`list::table_headers_ruled`**: `table_headers` with the line between the columns in the header only (the look
  of `Table`'s own header). Both go through one private `header_row`. Replaces `gz-ui-release`'s `local_table_head`.
- **`form::row_field(ui, salt, value, hint)`**: a `hint_field` as wide as the row (3 em at least) with its state under
  `Id::unique(salt)`. `form::hint_field` now reports a non-empty hint to the audit as the field's name (it was the Start
  page wrapper's one addition). Replaces `local_text_field` of `gz-ui-app` and `gz-ui-mod` and `local_hint_field` of
  `gz-ui-world`.
- **`state::spinner_at(ctx, painter, rect)`** is public (it was the dialog footer's private `paint_spinner`): a spinner
  inside a field or a list row. Replaces `gz-ui-world`'s `local_spinner_at`.
- `-kitsheet` shows the docked tabs, the flat strip and a spinner inside a field. Kit tests added: `tab_width`, the
  flat shrink, a docked tab's click / cross / right click (and a flat tab having no cross), a strip changing its
  choice by a click. `testkit::secondary_click_at` is new.

## What was removed

- `gz-ui-level/local.rs`: `local_tab`, `TabResponse`, `local_tab_width`.
- `gz-ui-pda`: `local_tab_strip`, and `drop.rs` whole (`begin_drops`, `local_file_drop_zone`, `simulate_drop`, the
  second board). Screens & Music takes its drops through `gz_ui::drop::Zone` (one private `take_files` at the top of
  `screens/doc.rs` turns a zone's payload into the paths the tab imports); `simulate_drop` of `screens.view` calls
  `gz_ui::drop::simulate_files`. The shell's one `gz_ui::drop::begin` starts the frame's drops; the tab no longer
  begins a board of its own. Payloads of `String` / `PathBuf` / `Vec<..>` the old copy also accepted had no sender
  anywhere, so they went.
- `gz-ui-world/local.rs`: `local_level_picker`, `open_level_picker` (World Rules' "Add Level..." is the kit's
  `PickList` through `pick_popup`, as Mod Settings does: hint "Search levels", the empty text "Link a game to pick its
  levels." or "No level matches."), `local_section` (7 callers: `Section` plus `ui.add_space(4.0)`), `local_hint_field`
  (4 callers in `char_form.rs` -> `row_field`), `local_check` (3 callers -> `form::check`), `local_cell_check` (one
  caller, inlined in `world.rs`), `local_spinner_at`, `set_section_open` (-> `section::set_open`).
- `gz-ui-app/local.rs`: `local_text_field` (one caller, `hint_field` with its id there). `gz-ui-mod/local.rs`:
  `local_text_field` (13 callers -> `row_field`).
- `gz-ui-release/local.rs`: `local_fold` (3 callers -> `Section` + a 2 pt / 4 pt gap), `local_table_head`
  (-> `table_headers_ruled`).
- Busy lines drawn by hand became the kit's `state::busy_line` (spinner, `ui.audit` `loading`): `submit.rs` "Reading
  the author key...", "Scanning...", "Looking the packages up on VirusTotal...", "Checking...", "Looking up the
  submission...", "Withdrawing..."; `publish.rs` "Reading the account..." (its "Contacting GitHub..." and the footer
  busy already were the kit's); `gz-ui-mod` `package.rs` "Reading the author key..." and `build.rs` "Reading
  mod.ltx..."; `gz-ui-app` `link.rs` "Looking at the folder..."; `gz-ui-level` `place.rs`'s "Reading the list..." note
  row (`Row::Note { busy }`: the kit's spinner in the row and the audit's `loading`; the JSON `note` text is unchanged).

`gz-ui-world/src/picker.rs` still holds `local_list_picker`, `local_combo`, `local_multi_combo` (not in `local.rs`, so
not in the brief): a long list in a popup with a search, a short list with a free preview, ticks over names. They are
the same job as the kit's `PickList` / `Combo` for the Characters tab and are the next merge candidate; I did not
touch them.

`gz-ui-level`'s dock keeps the drag-out-of-the-window logic around its tabs (`doc.rs`: a press on a tab let go outside
the window floats the panel) and the tab's context menu: they draw nothing and belong to the dock, not to the tab.

## Where the picture is not the old one

- Release windows: a fold was the property grid's section (a raised header with a hover fill, chevron at 6 pt, title
  at 24 pt); it is now the kit's `Section` (no fill, chevron at 2 pt, 28 pt) - the brief's decision, the kit wins.
  Notes (Releases), "More" (Submit key card), "Met (n)" (Requirements). The head of the Releases table keeps its column lines
  (`table_headers_ruled`; the Releases tab picture is identical).
- Busy lines are the kit's: a spinner, the text in the normal text colour instead of muted wrapped text (Submit's
  six lines, Publish's account row, Mod Settings' "Reading mod.ltx...", the author key row, Link Game's folder line, the
  Place list's "Reading the list...").
- World Rules' level list is the kit's `PickList` (ranked search, the kit's rows) instead of menu rows.
- A picker field's busy spinner (`gz-ui-world` `paint_field`) is the kit's spinner: a 2 pt stroke on a 1.5 pt-smaller
  radius instead of 1.5 pt / 1 pt.
- `hint_field` names itself to the audit by its hint: `ui.audit` lists more controls (no pixel changes).
- A mod text field's state id is `Id::unique(("gz-ui-mod.text", salt))` instead of the panel-relative persistent id.
